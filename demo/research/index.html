Fewer tasks can mean less precision.

Contiguous blocks reduce the number of query tasks. Nearby residuals are correlated, so matching label cost does not guarantee matching accuracy.

UNCERTAINTY

Accuracy and coverage are different questions.

An accurate point estimate can still have an unreliable normal interval. Conservative binary references cover the same prevalence with a separate center.

At the 40% budget, the weighted score method's nominal 95% normal interval covers only 39.1% of saved audits. Weighting improves point accuracy; it does not solve interval reliability.

Public error-allocation recipes

F · fixed queries

The primary inverse-fraction recipe narrows the score reference by 2.59% at 10%, below the registered 10% target. Weighted Bonferroni + exact hypergeometric stratum bounds is an established conservative construction. Its assumptions are a fixed population and valid within-stratum random sampling.

Read these intervals correctly

The binary reference need not contain the residual-correction point. It is not a replacement for the saved normal interval, and it does not apply to nonbinary block totals. G2's separately reported bounded residual-total intervals are conservative and broad. No intervals are intersected or selected after seeing a favorable draw.

METHOD & LIMITS

A reproducible course experiment.

The contribution is a working prevalence-audit system, careful budget comparisons, and an honest account of its useful and unsuccessful extensions.

FIXED-STRATUM DIFFERENCE ESTIMATOR

p̂ = λ mean(q) + Σₕ (Nₕ / M) meanₛₐₘₚₗₑ,ₕ(y − λq)

Freeze predictions and strata before querying labels. Randomly sample within each stratum, correct the prediction error, and retain finite-population variance. D selects λ on validation; test labels never select the D weight.

What the evidence supports

  • Benchmark-defined mutual-looking prevalence on 53 retained sources.
  • A modest weighting gain on the fixed 10,316-pair-frame test corpus.
  • Simple score controls, downstream label-cost curves, source sensitivity, and retained negative temporal/block findings.
  • Independent replay and numerical verification of the reported experiments.

What remains unresolved

  • Repeated inspection of the same test set limits confirmatory claims.
  • Only three validation sources; 53 of 95 attempted media sources retained.
  • Normal-interval undercoverage; conservative references remain broad.
  • No measured human annotation-time benefit or genuinely unseen-source evaluation yet.
  • No ADHD diagnosis, clinical assessment, live-camera system, or new statistical theorem.
