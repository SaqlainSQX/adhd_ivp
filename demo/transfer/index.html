POST-HOC INTERPRETATION · NO NEW WEIGHT FIT

A variance tradeoff, not a changed target.

Both estimators are design-unbiased. Reducing the model contribution changes residual variance. The balance between score variance and score–reference covariance differs across these two frozen populations.

V(0.5) − V(1) = B − 0.75 C

ChildPlay · half weight helps

B is smaller than 0.75 C. The variance reduction outweighs the lost covariance benefit.

UCO · half weight hurts

B is larger than 0.75 C. Reducing the model contribution loses more covariance benefit than it saves in variance.

A COURSE PROJECT WITH REVIEWABLE EVIDENCE

What we can honestly present.

“Score-guided sampling helped on a second benchmark, but the half-weight residual correction did not transfer.”

Scope of the result

  • UCO belongs to the pretrained backbone’s VSGaze family. This is downstream transfer, not unseen pretraining.
  • 15 clips from 4 TV shows are not independent evidence about new people or clinical populations.
  • UCO retains raw border-extending boxes; the ChildPlay export clipped boxes upstream.
  • The target is eligible annotated pair-frames, not prevalence throughout natural video.
  • This project does not diagnose ADHD.

Preparation and validation

  • An original one-based annotation ordinal required a documented zero-based mapping repair; the failed attempt is preserved.
  • 75 sampled frames across all 15 clips were inspected. Temporal identity screening is heuristic, not perfect track ground truth.
  • Unknown and ambiguous references never become negatives.
  • All 12,000 audit draws passed independent replay, including exact integer intervals and 12 bootstrap comparisons.
  • 16 saved exact variances and 96 stratum dot-product checks support the explanation.

Earlier findings remain part of the project

Prior temporal-feature, contiguous-block, source-sensitivity and interval-width failures remain in the original research explorer and tracked reports. The main study has not been replaced.
